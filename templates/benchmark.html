Understanding the Units
  • µs (Microseconds): $1\,\mu\text{s} = 0.000001$ seconds. The smaller the number, the faster it is!
  • Ops/Sec (Throughput): How many operations your CPU can execute in one second.
  • P95 Latency: 95% of operations finished faster than this threshold.
