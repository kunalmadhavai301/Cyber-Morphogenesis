How to Use This Platform (Quick Guide)

Welcome to the ML-KEM Benchmarking Platform! This website is designed to be very simple and intuitive. Follow these 4 straightforward steps to explore quantum-safe encryption:

Step 1
Try the Interactive Demo

Go to Demonstration to establish a quantum-safe key between Alice and Bob (Steps 1 → 4). Then, use the new Messaging Studio to encrypt and decrypt real messages with AES-256-GCM!

Open Demonstration
Step 2
Run a Live Benchmark

Go to Benchmark Lab to test the speed of ML-KEM on your computer. Select ML-KEM-768, pick 100 trials, and click "Start Benchmark". Microsecond charts will appear in seconds!

Open Benchmark Lab
Step 3
Compare All Parameters

Go to Comparison to visualize the trade-offs between ML-KEM-512, 768, and 1024, plus see how post-quantum lattice crypto compares to classical Elliptic Curve (ECDH).

Open Comparison
Step 4
Export & Print Report

Inspect past runs in History, download datasets as CSV for Excel or research, or visit Report to view and print a publication-ready academic report.

View Academic Report
