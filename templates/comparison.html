What Do These Charts Tell You? (Key Takeaways)
⚡ Speed is Very Fast A common myth is that post-quantum crypto is slow. In reality, ML-KEM operations execute in under $100\,\mu\text{s}$ (0.0001 seconds), matching or beating many classical algorithms!
📦 Key Size is the Real Trade-Off Classical keys (ECDH) are tiny (32–64 bytes). ML-KEM keys and ciphertexts are larger (~1 to 2 Kilobytes) due to high-dimensional polynomial matrices.
🛡️ Quantum Immune Classical curves will be broken by Shor's quantum algorithm. ML-KEM is built on the Module-LWE lattice problem, which quantum computers cannot break in polynomial time.
